This is my second lab project of Web Development class
In this lab i use header, main, articles and footer tag to structure my page.

What I Learned

  • I got confused between block and inline elements at first.
    I didn't really understand why some things went to a new line and others stayed on the same line, until I actually tried it myself and saw the difference.
  • I also had trouble knowing when to use section vs article.
    They felt like the same thing to me at first, but once I looked into it more I realized article is for content that can stand on its own, while section is more just for grouping stuff together.

  2. W3C Validator

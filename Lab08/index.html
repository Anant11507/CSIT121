Welcome

I picked Dashain as my theme because it is a big Hindu festival that is coming up soon. Families get together, eat food and get tika from the elders. Dashain, also called Vijaya Dashami or Bada Dashain, is Nepal longest and most important Hindu festival, lasting 15 days and celebrating the victory of good over evil through the worship of Goddess Durga and other mythological triumphs

The background is a photo of puja plates with apples, tika rice and jamara.
